$$
\frac{\partial\Phi_2}{\partial\lambda}
=P_AQ_{A2}+P_BQ_{B2}-\pi_B-rL_B=0
$$

— бюджетне обмеження.

Звідси:

$$
\frac{P_A}{P_B}
=\frac{\dfrac{3(Q_{B2}-4)^{0.6}(16-L_B)^{0.1}}{10(Q_{A2}-8)^{0.7}}}
{\dfrac{3(Q_{A2}-8)^{0.3}(16-L_B)^{0.1}}{5(Q_{B2}-4)^{0.4}}}
=\frac{Q_{B2}-4}{2(Q_{A2}-8)}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
$$

$$
Q_{B2}-4=\frac{2(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{P_B}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
Q_{B2}=\frac{2(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{P_B}+4.
$$

$$
\frac{P_A}{r}
=\frac{\dfrac{3(Q_{B2}-4)^{0.6}(16-L_B)^{0.1}}{10(Q_{A2}-8)^{0.7}}}
{\dfrac{(Q_{A2}-8)^{0.3}(Q_{B2}-4)^{0.6}}{10(16-L_B)^{0.9}}}
=\frac{3(16-L_B)}{Q_{A2}-8}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
$$

$$
3(16-L_B)=\frac{(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{r}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
L_B=16-\frac{(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{3r}.
$$

Підстановка в бюджетне обмеження дає:

$$
P_AQ_{A2}+P_B\left(\frac{2(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{P_B}+4\right)-\pi_B-r\left(16-\frac{(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{3r}\right)=0;
$$

$$
P_AQ_{A2}+2(Q_{A2}-8)P_A+4P_B-\pi_B-16r+\frac{(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{3}=0;
$$

$$
3P_AQ_{A2}+6P_AQ_{A2}-48P_A+12P_B-3\pi_B-48r+P_AQ_{A2}-8P_A=0;
$$

$$
10P_AQ_{A2}-56P_A+12P_B-3\pi_B-48r=0,
$$

$$
10P_AQ_{A2}=56P_A-12P_B+3\pi_B+48r.
$$

отже:

$$
Q_{A2}^D=5.6+\frac{0.3\pi_B+4.8r-1.2P_B}{P_A}.
$$

$$
Q_{B2}^D=\frac{2\left(5.6+\frac{0.3\pi_B+4.8r-1.2P_B}{P_A}-8\right)P_A}{P_B}+4
=\frac{0.6\pi_B+9.6r-2.4P_B-4.8P_A}{P_B}+4
=1.6+\frac{0.6\pi_B+9.6r-4.8P_A}{P_B}.
$$

$$
L_B^S=16-\frac{(Q_{A2}-8)P_A}{3r}
=16-\frac{\left(5.6+\frac{0.3\pi_B+4.8r-1.2P_B}{P_A}-8\right)P_A}{3r}
=16-\frac{0.3\pi_B+4.8r-1.2P_B-2.4P_A}{3r}
